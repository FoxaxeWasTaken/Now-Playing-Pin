# Safety and wearing

Status: rules written before any part exists. They are reviewed when the first pin is built.

The pin is a home-made object with a lithium cell and magnets, worn on clothing. It is not
a certified product.

## Battery

- Only a cell with a built-in protection circuit.
- No pressure point on the cell, and room for it to swell.
- Check the connector polarity with a multimeter before the first connection.
- Do not charge the pin while wearing it. Charge it on a hard surface, in sight.
- Stop using a cell that is swollen, dented or that has been pierced.
- Do not leave the pin in a car in the sun.

## Magnets

- Keep the magnet clip at least 15 cm from a pacemaker or an implanted defibrillator. A pin
  worn on the chest is too close for someone who has one.
- Keep it away from magnetic-stripe cards.
- The magnets are strong enough to pinch skin.

## Temperature and water

- The panel is specified for 0 to 50 °C in operation. In the cold, a refresh may be slow or
  incomplete.
- The case is not sealed. Rain and sweat can reach the board through the button openings and
  the USB port. Take the pin off in the rain.
- PETG softens in strong heat. See the note about cars above.

## Falls

- The panel is glass about 1 mm thick. A fall face down can break it.
- The magnet clip holds less well on thick fabric. The pin is tried on the clothes it is
  meant for before it is trusted outdoors.

## Checks before wearing the first pin

- [ ] Weight of the finished pin, with and without the clip.
- [ ] Hold on thin and thick fabric, standing and moving.
- [ ] One fall from waist height onto a hard floor, in the case, before the real panel is
      fitted if a spare is not available.
- [ ] Temperature of the case after a full charge.
