# ADR-0004: nRF52840 board (EN05) rather than ESP32-S3 (EE05)

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-19 (recorded; the decision is older)
- Related: [hardware.md](../hardware.md), [power.md](../power.md)

## Context

The Seeed XIAO ePaper Display Board comes in two variants: the EN05 with a XIAO nRF52840
Plus, and the EE05 with an ESP32-S3. Both carry the display connector, battery connector,
charger and buttons that the pin needs.

Nothing has been ordered. The comparison rests on published information, not on measurements.

## Decision

The pin uses the EN05 (nRF52840).

Reasons:

- BLE power consumption, which decides the battery life ([ADR-0003](0003-ble-only-pin-as-peripheral.md)).
- NFC, needed for the "tap to play" feature planned for V2.

## Alternatives

- EE05 (ESP32-S3). Brings Wi-Fi, which the pin must not use, and not the two points above.

## Consequences

- Firmware is built with the Seeed nRF52 Arduino core and Bluefruit
  ([ADR-0014](0014-arduino-seeed-nrf52-core.md)).
- The XIAO's QSPI flash must be put in deep power-down explicitly, or it keeps drawing current.
- Board dimensions and the display configuration are still **[to verify]**.
- The board may carry no NFC antenna, only the two lines **[to verify]**. NFC would then need
  an external coil.
- The idle current of the complete board is not documented, and it weighs on the battery
  life as much as the choice of chip.
- The choice costs nothing to revisit until the board is ordered.
